Sacred Bonds — Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 10, 2026

Publisher: CristoVieneProntoby_deborah
Privacy contact: cristovieneprontobydeborah@gmail.com

Your love. Your faith. Your privacy.

The moments you share with God and each other belong to you. This policy explains what stays on your device and the choices you have.

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Sacred Bonds mobile app for couples building a faith-centered relationship. “We” refers to CristoVieneProntoby_deborah, the publisher of Sacred Bonds. The app works without an account, a developer-operated server, or a cloud connection. The app-store listing and any website hosting this policy are separate services.

2. What you choose to save

On your device, the app saves the partner names, relationship stage, anniversary date and optional church-service weekdays you enter. It also saves prayer titles, prayer text, prayer topics and answered status; faith goals and their progress; devotional completions; saved Bible passages and date ideas; activity checkoffs and history; guided-prayer sessions; worship and reading streaks; language, theme, reading-size and onboarding preferences. Completion dates support your history and progress. Your journal may reveal personal relationships or religious beliefs, so write only what you feel comfortable storing.

3. How your information is used

The app reads this local information only to personalize your couple profile, reopen your journal, calculate your anniversary countdown, remember saved content and display your progress and chosen settings. Bible passages, devotional content and images are bundled with the app. Search, daily verse selection and progress calculations happen on your device. Changing the app language does not upload or rewrite your personal entries.

4. No collection, sale or tracking

The app does not send your journal, profile or activity data to us or to other people. We do not collect, sell, rent or share this local information. There are no advertising, analytics, tracking or remote AI features in the app, and it does not synchronize information between partners. We do not keep a server copy of your entries and cannot view or recover them.

5. No camera, microphone or calendar

The app does not request or use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, photos or media library. Church weekdays and anniversaries are entered manually and do not connect to your phone calendar. Core features do not require an internet connection. Bible-source addresses on the credits page are attribution text, not automatic downloads.

6. Local storage and device security

Information is kept in app-scoped local storage (AsyncStorage). The app does not add its own password lock or database encryption. Your device’s operating system controls access and device-level protection. Use a device passcode and keep your device secure; someone with access to an unlocked device may be able to read your journal. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Retention, editing and deletion

Entries stay in local app storage until you remove them, delete all app data, or remove the app’s local storage. Edit Profile lets you change your names, relationship stage, anniversary and service days. Erase couple profile removes those details while keeping your journal and progress. Individual prayers and goals can be deleted. Delete all my app data removes the saved app-data record and resets the app to its welcome screen. This action cannot be undone, and we cannot restore your entries.

8. Uninstalling, backups and previews

Deleting the mobile app normally removes its active local data, but offloading an app is not the same as deleting it. Android backup is disabled in the app configuration. iOS device backups, system transfers and any existing backups follow the operating system’s settings; uninstalling does not guarantee deletion of those copies. Manage them in your device or backup-provider settings. In a browser preview, data is stored by the browser, not by a mobile installation, and must be cleared separately. Hosting or development-preview services may handle technical connection data under their own policies. These are not app journal-upload features.

9. Intended audience

Sacred Bonds is designed for adult couples, not directed to children under 13. We do not operate a registration service or collect children’s information on a server. Anyone using a shared device should avoid recording another person’s sensitive information without their permission.

10. App stores and your choices

Apple, Google and your device or backup provider may process store-account, download, diagnostic or backup information under their own policies and your device settings. This policy describes the app itself, not those independent services. You control the information you enter and can review, edit or delete it locally. Because we do not hold your journal or profile, there is no server account to delete or server copy for us to provide.

11. Questions and policy changes

For privacy questions, contact CristoVieneProntoby_deborah at cristovieneprontobydeborah@gmail.com. If you email us, your email provider and ours process the message, and we use the information you send to respond to your request. Do not send private journal entries when asking a question. If the app’s privacy practices change, this policy and its date will be updated. Any future feature that sends information off your device must be disclosed before you use it.
