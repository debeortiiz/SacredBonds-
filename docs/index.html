Privacy first

Your personal entries stay on your device.

No login. No cloud account. No camera, microphone, location, or calendar access.

Private by design

Your relationship is personal. Your journal should be, too. Sacred Bonds does not require an account or upload your personal entries to an app server.

No automatic sharing

Each partner uses their own device and enters information separately. There is no automatic sharing, no account linking, and no cloud sync.

You’re in control

Use the app’s settings to erase your couple profile or all saved app information. You decide what to discuss and share with each other.

For details about local storage, backups, and deletion, read the full Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

A few helpful answers.

Who is Sacred Bonds for?

Sacred Bonds welcomes married, engaged, and other couples who want to grow closer to God and one another through Christian spiritual practices and intentional connection.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app opens directly to Home. Adding names and creating a couple profile are optional.

Does it work without internet?

The app’s built-in content and personal tracking features work offline after installation. Downloading the app or installing store updates requires internet access.

Will my partner automatically see my entries?

No. Devices are not linked. Partners enter information separately and share through conversation if they choose.

Does it contain a complete Bible?

No. Sacred Bonds currently includes a curated collection of 31 Scripture passages in each supported language, not a complete Bible. Search applies to that included collection.

Can I choose our church meeting days?

Yes. You can select any combination of weekdays in your couple profile. No Sunday assumption or calendar permission is required.

How do I delete my information?

Use the privacy controls in Settings to erase your couple profile or all locally saved app information.
