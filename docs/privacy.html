Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 12, 2026

App operator: Cristo Viene Pronto By Deborah
Contact: cristovieneprontobydeborah@gmail.com

Your love. Your faith. Your privacy. This policy explains what Sacred Bonds stores on your device and the choices you have. It covers the mobile app, not the separate app-store services or the hosting provider for this website.

1. Information stored on your device

Sacred Bonds may store information you enter locally on your device, including optional couple profile details, prayer entries, faith goals, bookmarks, activity history, progress, and preferences. This information remains on the device unless you choose to erase it.

2. Collection and sharing

The app does not require registration and does not transmit these personal entries to the app operator. It does not include in-app advertising, analytics, or automatic partner synchronization. Each partner uses their own device and enters information separately.

3. Device access

The app does not request or use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or calendar. Anniversaries and church weekdays are entered manually and do not connect to your phone calendar.

4. Deleting information

Settings provides options to erase your couple profile or all saved app information. These controls remove the relevant information from the app’s active local storage. Deletion cannot be undone. Because the app does not keep a server copy, the app operator cannot recover your entries.

Device backups, app offloading, and device restoration are controlled by your operating system. Sacred Bonds cannot erase copies held in existing system backups.

5. Support messages

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send will be received by the support contact. Avoid sending private journal entries or sensitive relationship information through email.

6. Website and platform services

This policy describes the Sacred Bonds app. Apple, Google, and your device or backup provider may process account, download, diagnostic, or backup information under their own policies and settings.

7. Policy updates

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated version.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact cristovieneprontobydeborah@gmail.com.
